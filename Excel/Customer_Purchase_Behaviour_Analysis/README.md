# 🛒 Customer Purchase Behavior Dashboard

## 📌 Project Objective
**The objective of this project** was to analyze customer purchase behavior in an e-commerce dataset to identify **high-value customer segments**, **spending patterns**, and **product category trends** in order to support **targeted marketing and revenue optimization strategies**.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
- **Pivot Tables & Charts**
- **Data Cleaning & Grouping**
- **Conditional Formatting**

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Income range 35k–45k shows a high concentration of frequent purchasers**
- **Electronics is the highest-selling category with many frequent buyers**
- **Age group 22–31 primarily falls under frequent purchasing behavior**
- Loyalty status influences purchase consistency, with higher tiers showing more Frequent buyers

## 🔍 Next Steps / Recommendations
- Develop retention strategies to convert **Occasional shoppers into Frequent buyers**
- Target the **35k–45k income segment** with loyalty incentives
- Create promotional campaigns around **Electronics**, leveraging its popularity
- Personalize marketing efforts for **22–31 age group**
- Encourage repeat purchases from mid-range spenders (10k–13k) through bundle offers or discounts
