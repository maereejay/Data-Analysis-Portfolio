# Chinook Music Store SQL Analysis

## Project Overview
This project demonstrates an end-to-end **SQL and Python analysis** of the Chinook digital music store database.  
The goal is to explore **customer behavior, revenue trends, and top-selling products** to extract actionable business insights.

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQLite**: Querying and aggregating the database
- **Python (pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)**: Data analysis and visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook**: Interactive workflow and documentation
- **GitHub**: Project version control and portfolio showcase

---

## Database
- **Chinook SQLite database** (`Chinook.db`)
- Tables used include:
  - `customers`
  - `invoices`
  - `invoice_items`
  - `tracks`
  - `genres`

---

## Analyses & Insights

1. **Customer Distribution**
   - Number of customers by country
   - Insight: USA has the highest number of customers, followed by other countries.

2. **Revenue Analysis**
   - Total revenue by country and by genre
   - Insight: Rock, Latin, and Metal dominate revenue, with some genres earning from multiple tracks.

3. **Top-Spending Customers**
   - Identified top 10 customers by total spend
   - Insight: High-value spenders may not always be the most frequent purchasers.

4. **Top-Selling Tracks**
   - Identified tracks with highest units sold
   - Insight: Rock and Metal tracks dominate the top-selling list.

5. **Revenue and Purchases Over Time**
   - Monthly and yearly revenue trends
   - Average purchases per month across all years
   - Insight: Highlights seasonal trends and customer activity patterns.

6. **Customer Purchase Frequency**
   - Top customers by number of purchases
   - Insight: Many customers are tied at 7 purchases, showing distributed loyalty.

---

## Visualizations
All analyses are visualized with **Matplotlib and Seaborn**. Examples include:
- Bar plots: Customers by country, top spenders, top-selling tracks
- Line plots: Monthly revenue trends, average purchases per month
